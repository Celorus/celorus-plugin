# The source ladder

Ordered, with what each rung yields and how far to trust it. Stop when the page's
sections are filled or the ladder is exhausted. Every line written from a rung carries
`web · unverified · <url> · <date read>`; a rung's tier below is shown on the line when
it is below `demonstrated`.

## Companies

| Rung | Source | Yields | Tier | Trust |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exchange announcements (the BSE and NSE corporate-announcement pages), listed companies only | dated events: results, board changes, allotments, buybacks | `filed` on the exchange; still `web` on the page | strongest; the company's own filing to the exchange |
| 2 | Credit-rating rationales (CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, India Ratings), rated companies | real figures, dated, with the analyst's reading; ownership and group facts | `reported` | the best free source of figures; still not the record |
| 3 | The registrar's master-data lookup (company status, incorporation, directors' names) | identity, status, the board's names | `registered` | reliable identity; sign-in requirements change, so say when the lookup could not be reached |
| 4 | Tax and enterprise registrations (GST and Udyam lookups) | existence, state, registration date | `registered` | existence only; never a figure |
| 5 | Quality financial press (Mint, The Economic Times, Business Standard, Moneycontrol; VCCircle and Inc42 for funding) | funding events, leadership changes, disputes | `reported` | one paper is medium confidence; two are a line |
| 6 | The company's own site and press page | what it says about itself | `stated` | self-published; shown with its tier |

Never on the ladder: aggregator previews of figures with no named source, forum posts,
anonymous listings.

## People and families

| Rung | Source | Yields | Tier | Trust |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The firm's own team or leadership page | exact current title, a stated philosophy | `stated` | best; fetches cleanly |
| 2 | Appointment and leadership press releases | the mandate, the scope, the career history, a quote from whoever hired them | `reported` | best for the mandate |
| 3 | Trade-press interviews with the person or the founders | strategy, stated hardest problem | `stated` | high value; many block fetching, so the search summary is often all there is |
| 4 | Professional registers, where the profession has one | qualification, registration | `registered` | clean identity for professionals |
| 5 | Contact aggregators (corroborated by a second source) | titles, sometimes stale | `affiliated` | never alone; medium confidence until corroborated |
| 6 | The professional-network profile, through the search summary only (the page will not fetch) | headline, current employer | `stated` | the summary is the accessible part; say so |

A family page is built from its principals' pages: each principal researched on this
ladder; the family's holdings and household aggregate only from the record; nothing about
a relationship from a surname, a locality or a co-appearance.

## The discipline

- Two independent sources or the line is medium confidence.
- A figure carries its URL and date or is not written.
- The record's citation vocabulary never appears in a `web` line.
- What the ladder could not establish goes under "Not established", by name, so the
  reader knows what is missing rather than assuming it is absent.
